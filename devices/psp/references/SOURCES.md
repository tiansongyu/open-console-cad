# Sony PSP-1000: source basis

- [Official product specification](https://sonyinteractive.com/uploads/sites/5/2023/02/040512a.pdf)
- [Firsthand teardown / repair reference](https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/PSP+1000+Teardown/1234)

User-selected original model: PSP-1000. Reference review 2026-09-08. Overall body dimensions: 170 × 74 × 23 mm (manufacturer convention; PSP/Vita exclude largest protrusions).

Local contours, internal modules and accessory dimensions are approximate. Photographs are used locally and not redistributed.

- [Sony PSP-1000 original Japanese instruction manual](https://www.playstation.com/content/dam/global_pdc/en/corporate/support/manuals/psp-docs/JA_PSP-1000_WEB.pdf), parts and functions pp. 20–23, battery specification pp. 116–118.

The 2004 product specification explicitly excludes maximum projections from the 170 × 74 × 23 mm envelope. This study reports body-only and overall modeled dimensions separately. The 4.3-inch 16:9 window is calculated geometrically. The UMD shell and disc use the published 65 × 64 × 4.2 mm and 60 mm diameter as reference; local openings and mechanical details remain approximate. The community teardown is used as photographic layout evidence, not for its unverified chip-function or radio claims.

## 2026-11-08 pack and interface correction

- [Sony launch release, 2004-10-27, p.3](https://sonyinteractive.com/uploads/sites/5/2023/02/041027a.pdf): PSP-110 approximately 52 × 12.5 × 36 mm, excluding maximum projections. Installed model axes are 36 × 52 × 12.5 mm. Corner curvature and internal cell geometry remain estimated.
- [Original Sony parts guide, pp.20–22 and 32](https://www.playstation.com/content/dam/global_pdc/en/corporate/support/manuals/psp-docs/JA_PSP-1000_WEB.pdf): HOLD indicator and original port placement. The model presents the unlocked flag.
- [Firsthand PSP remote-interface experiment](https://nil.rpc1.org/psp/remote.html) and [original hardware pinout documentation](https://hitmen.c02.at/files/yapspd/psp_doc/chap3.html): six remote contacts in two rows of three. This is the original PSP-1000 interface.
- [Neubit's firsthand LED replacement guide](https://acidmods.com/forum/index.php/topic,27375.0.html): orange Memory Stick activity indicator. Rendering colors are illustrative.

[fidelity_iteration16.json](fidelity_iteration16.json) distinguishes the published pack dimensions, observed connector topology and estimated mechanical details. The repositioned reader, control stack and frame relief remain schematic.
