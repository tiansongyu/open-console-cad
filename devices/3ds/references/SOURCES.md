# Nintendo 3DS CTR-001 reference basis

## 2026-11-08 fidelity review

- [Nintendo CTR-001 manual](https://www.nintendo.com.hk/3ds/CTR_manual.pdf), printed pages 20 and 22: notification light, 3D indicator, wireless indicator, outer-camera activity light and two strap attachment openings. Pages 32 and 100 identify the full-size SD card and published system envelope.
- [SD Association form factors](https://www.sdcard.org/developers/sd-standard-overview/capacity-sd-sdhc-sdxc-sduc/): standard SD card envelope of 32 × 24 × 2.1 mm.

Iteration 15 corrects the undersized SD card and side-entry contact arrangement. The socket, carrier and local shield/frame clearances remain schematic. Indicator and strap dimensions were fitted to the diagrams; no factory molding tolerances or specimen measurements are claimed. See [fidelity_iteration15.json](fidelity_iteration15.json) for per-feature evidence and limits.

- [Nintendo 3DS official manual](https://www.nintendo.com.hk/3ds/CTR_manual.pdf), pp. 20–23 and 100–101: closed envelope 134 × 74 × 21 mm; upper active display 76.8 × 46.08 mm; lower active display 61.44 × 46.08 mm. CTR-001 original model, not New 3DS or XL.
- [iFixit 2011 Nintendo 3DS teardown](https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/Nintendo+3DS+Teardown/5029): firsthand exterior and major internal module references. Battery CTR-003, 1300 mAh / 5 Wh; removable Wi-Fi, IR, Circle Pad and SD modules; upper LCD, speaker assembly and three-camera flex.

Source review: 2026-09-08. Model color: Aqua Blue interpretation. Exterior contours, local wall/connector dimensions, mounting details and internal component dimensions are approximate. Opening angle is a presentation parameter. Reference photographs stay local and are not redistributed. Original reference dimensions refer to the closed console.
