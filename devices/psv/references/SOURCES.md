# PlayStation Vita: source basis

- [Official product specification](https://sonyinteractive.com/en/corporate/release/2011/111019b.html)
- [Firsthand teardown / repair reference](https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/PlayStation+Vita+Teardown/7872)

User-selected original model: PCH-1000 Wi-Fi. Reference review 2026-09-08. Overall body dimensions: 182 × 83.5 × 18.6 mm (manufacturer convention; PSP/Vita exclude largest protrusions).

Local contours, internal modules and accessory dimensions are approximate. Photographs are used locally and not redistributed.

- [Sony PCH-1000/PCH-1100 original quick-start guide](https://www.playstation.com/content/dam/global_pdc/en/corporate/support/manuals/psvita-docs/JA_PCH-1000_1100-1.03_2_WEB.pdf): original card covers, charging connector and model differences.

The selected model is PCH-1000 Wi-Fi OLED. The iFixit unit is a PCH-1100-series 3G model; its cellular card, SIM board and GPS-specific hardware are excluded from this study. Common enclosure, dual controls, OLED, camera, rear-touch and battery layouts are used as photographic references. Tiny printed symbols and connector contacts are simplified visual studies, not electrical pinouts.
